# Banned Patterns

The "generic AI look". Stage 04 self-checks its design against this list, and stage 06 reviews the
screenshots against it. A run adds to it through `avoid` in `_config/run.md`; it never removes an entry.

1. Purple, indigo, or violet-to-blue gradients; gradient text; glowing or blurred "orb" backgrounds
2. A centered hero with a big headline, a subhead, and a pill button
3. Grids of identical rounded cards with emoji or generic line icons
4. Glassmorphism, heavy drop shadows, neon glows
5. Sparkle motifs, "AI-powered" badges, rocket or lightning iconography
6. A single system typeface with no typographic hierarchy
7. Marketing filler: "seamless", "unlock", "supercharge", "next-generation", "revolutionize", "effortless"
8. Decorative motion: floating elements, parallax, constant pulsing
9. Stock-style 3D illustrations or abstract blobs
10. Freehand diagrams that bypass the system model
